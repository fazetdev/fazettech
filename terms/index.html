Terms of Service.

The legal framework governing our engineering and AI consultation partnerships.

1. Engagement & Services

▼

Fazet Tech provides custom software engineering and AI consultation. All project specifics, timelines, and deliverables are defined in individual Service Agreements signed by both parties. These online terms provide a general framework for our professional conduct.

✓ Defined by contract.

2. Intellectual Property (IP)

▼

Unless otherwise stated in a specific contract, all code and architectural designs remain the property of Fazet Tech until full and final payment is received. Upon completion and payment, the client is granted the rights to the production-ready code for their specific use case.

✓ Protection of authorship.

3. AI Output & Liability

▼

For AI-driven solutions, Fazet Tech provides the infrastructure and grounding logic. We are not responsible for the specific real-time "outputs" generated by third-party LLMs. We implement RAG to maximize accuracy, but absolute output control is restricted by the nature of generative models.

✓ Controlled Intelligence.

4. Payment & Milestones

▼

We operate on a "One Step at a Time" milestone basis. Invoices are issued per completed stage. Work on subsequent stages will only commence once the previous milestone is approved and settled.

✓ Milestone-based progress.
